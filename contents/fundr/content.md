## Problem

### Why Fundr needed a redesign

Fundr needed to monetize without alienating users, but the product had no upgrade path.

- Every plan change ran through a 1:1 sales call - users waited days for a human to flip a switch.
- The pricing was confusing, and the low tiers did not justify upgrading.
- There was no self-service path to upgrade.

I had ten weeks to design one.

Three things standing in the way

1. Sales calls for every upgrade
2. Confusing pricing structure
3. Free tier too limited to sell upgrades

"I didn't even know there were paid plans. I've been on the same tier since I signed up."

User during discovery interviews

## Business Context

Pricing affects the bottom line nearly 4x over acquisition. Monetization has a 12.7% impact on the bottom line compared to 3.32% from customer acquisition.

Patrick Campbell

This reframed the project from "UI cleanup" to a business-critical decision. Pricing surface was the lever - not polish.

## Solution

### The Upgrade Flow

Every path leads to checkout - here is the full picture.

Before diving into individual surfaces, it is worth seeing how the pieces connect. The upgrade flow has three entry points - a scrollable sales funnel, feature-specific popups, and situational upgrade prompts - all converging into a single checkout. Users can start from anywhere and finish in under a minute.
