What a fun fun weekend building this.

**Memory-ando** is a game where you take photos in the moment and turn them into a **memory match game**. It's designed to keep you **grounded and aware** of your surroundings - because sometimes the best brain training is just paying attention to what's right in front of you.

The inspiration? My **grandma** kept forgetting whether she'd taken her pill. (And she had the most hilarious comments about it, like wondering if she was drinking it without noticing. She's a gem.)

I was living with her at the time - job searching, doing a fast, learning piano, practicing Italian, jumping rope, remodeling my bedroom. Basically filling the void with hobbies (lol). Seeing Jack Bernstein's posts made me realize I didn't need a job to keep designing. I could just build something for my grandma.

So I did. Memory-ando is in **Spanish by default** - gotta make it easy for my Latina grandma - with an English toggle. Built in a weekend, **designed for family.**

## Highlights

- Take photos in the moment and turn them into a memory game
- Spanish-first interface with English toggle
- Built in a weekend for a real personal need
- Designed to be shared with family across generations
