A website design project for **Jimmy Nabors Pickleball**, a USA Pickleball certified coach and referee in Bakersfield, CA. The goal was a bold, energetic site that makes it easy for players to see the services and book a session.

## Highlights

- Website design and build for a certified coach and referee
- Deployed on Vercel
- Clear, mobile-friendly layout for players
